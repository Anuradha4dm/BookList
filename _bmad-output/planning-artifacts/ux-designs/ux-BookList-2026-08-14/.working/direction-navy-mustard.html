Book List Direction A Navy & school-bag mustard

Sturdy, signposted, unmistakably school.

This direction makes the strongest case for the palette the PRD left unresolved: deep navy carries every piece of chrome and every number, and school-bag mustard is reserved for the one thing you are meant to touch. Shapes are chunky and physically weighted — 2–3px edges, hard offset shadows, buttons that look pressable — so a parent thumbing through a long book list on a phone in the evening never has to hunt for the control. It serves Nimali under time pressure and a vendor who wants a screen that looks built, not sketched: confidence you can see from arm's length.

Accessibility, stated not assumed

  • Body text is navy #0B1B33 on cream #FFFBF2 at 14.5:1; supporting text #4A5A72 at 6.8:1.
  • The primary button carries navy text on mustard at 7.3:1. White on mustard is 2.1:1 and is banned in this direction — that is the trap the palette sets, and this is how it is avoided.
  • Every control boundary uses #8A7C61 at 4.0:1 against cream, clearing the 3:1 floor. The pale #D7CDB8 rule is decorative and never outlines a control.
  • Focus is a 3px #1B3A66 ring with a 2px offset gap so it reads on cream, on white and on mustard. The atlas checkbox in the hero shows it in its resting focused state.
  • Checkboxes and stepper buttons all sit in 44×44px targets; primary actions are 52px tall.
  • No state is carried by colour alone: "Not called yet" is a hollow navy ring, "Called · no answer" is a filled amber chip with a timestamp, and both are labelled in words.
  • Motion implication: short, weighted transitions — a 120ms press that flattens the offset shadow, and totals that count rather than jump. Nothing that delays the vendor's call or Nimali's checkout, and all of it dropped under prefers-reduced-motion.
