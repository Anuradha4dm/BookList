Book List Direction B Warm shopfront

The shop that already knows your child's grade.

Warm paper, a terracotta awning stripe, and a serif that behaves like a printed booklist: this direction borrows the register of a small family bookshop rather than a marketplace. Rows breathe, rules are dotted rather than drawn, and nothing shouts — the reassurance is doing the selling, because Nimali is handing over her address and her children's names to a shop she has only spoken to on the phone. It serves the trust problem first and still costs the vendor nothing in clarity, because every price sits on white.

Accessibility, stated not assumed

  • Body ink #2C1F17 on paper #FDF8F0 is 15.2:1; supporting #6B5849 is 6.3:1. Warmth is in the hue, not in a loss of contrast — this is the trap "cosy" palettes usually fall into.
  • The primary button is white on terracotta #A8412A at 6.1:1, so it holds at any size; terracotta as text on paper is 5.7:1.
  • Every control boundary uses #9A7E62 at 3.6:1 against paper, over the 3:1 UI floor. The pale #E0CFB8 dotted rules are decorative and never outline a control.
  • Focus is a 3px terracotta ring with a 2px offset, legible on paper, on white cards and on the terracotta buttons themselves. The atlas checkbox in the hero is shown in its focused state.
  • Serif is used for headings, prices and titles only. Editions, timestamps and microcopy stay in the system sans at 12.5px and above, so nothing small is also stylised.
  • All checkboxes, steppers and row actions are 44×44px or larger; primary buttons are 48px.
  • Tags never rely on hue: "Nobody has rung yet" is dashed with a hollow marker, "Rang 9:15 am · no answer" is filled with a time in it.
  • Motion implication: things settle rather than snap — 220ms ease-out fades, a row that softens when unticked instead of disappearing. All of it withdrawn under prefers-reduced-motion, and never in front of the vendor's call.
