Colour themes, pass 1 — four refinements of navy & school-bag mustard

The direction is settled; this pass only argues about register. Each variation keeps navy as the structural ink and mustard as the brand action, and changes three things: how warm the light surface is, how much structural work mustard is allowed to do, and what the dark base actually is. Light and dark sit side by side because parents order in the evening — dark is a peer, not an inversion. Every chip states its hex and its measured contrast ratio against the surface it is intended to sit on.

How the eight statuses stay separable

Hue is the fourth signal, never the first. Every pill carries a step number (1–8, with × for the terminal cancel), a unique glyph (dashed ring, tick, half-disc, crate, flag, arrow, double tick, cross), and a weight band — dashed outline for not-yet-started, tint or outline for in-flight, solid fill for the two states that end the day. Terminal states also change shape: square-cut corners against the in-flight pills. Photocopied in greyscale, or read by a deuteranope, the ladder still reads in order.
