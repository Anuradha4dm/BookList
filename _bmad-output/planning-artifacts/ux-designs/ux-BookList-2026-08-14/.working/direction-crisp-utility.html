Book List Direction C Crisp utility Density: high

A tool, not a shop window. Twenty seconds, in and out.

Tight leading, a monospaced numeral for every figure, hairline rules instead of cards, and one indigo reserved for the thing that commits. Everything is on screen at once so nothing has to be remembered: Nimali sees the whole list and the running total without scrolling past decoration, and the vendor sees eleven orders, six counts and the confirm field in a single glance with keyboard paths through all of it. Density here comes from type and rules — not from shrinking hit areas, which stay at 44px.

Accessibility, stated not assumed

  • #101828 on white is 17.6:1. Because this direction leans on 10.5–12px labels, the greys are kept dark on purpose: #344054 is 10.3:1 and #475467 is 7.6:1, so even the smallest label clears 4.5:1 comfortably.
  • White on indigo #3538CD is 8.1:1; indigo as text on white is the same figure.
  • Every control boundary is #667085 at 4.9:1 on white. #D0D5DD hairlines are decorative table rules only and never outline a control — that distinction is what lets the tables look this thin and still pass.
  • Focus is a 2px indigo ring plus a 2px offset, giving a 4px visible band. The atlas checkbox in the hero is shown focused.
  • Density is not achieved by shrinking targets. Rows are 44px tall because the controls inside them are; the compression comes from type scale, 1.42 leading and 1px rules. Stepper buttons are 36×44px and sit inside a 44px row.
  • The selected admin row carries an inset 3px indigo bar in addition to its wash, so keyboard position is not colour-only. "Never called" is dashed with ○, "Attempted 09:15" is solid amber with ● and a time.
  • Keyboard paths are shown, not implied: JK, C, A, R on admin; Space, ↑↓, +− on the pack table.
  • Motion implication: 80–120ms, functional only — a figure that ticks over, a row that shades. Fast enough to be invisible, which is the point (NFR7). Dropped entirely under prefers-reduced-motion.
